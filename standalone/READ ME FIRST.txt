SHIUR PLAYER 0.9.2 — WINDOWS INSTALLER AND IN-APP UPDATES

ONE-TIME SETUP
Close the older player. Run shiur-player-setup-0.9.2.exe and click OK.
There is no ZIP to extract. Setup creates desktop and Start menu shortcuts.
No administrator access, Python installation or VLC installation is needed.
Use the Shiur Player shortcut to open the app on future days.

FUTURE UPDATES
Click Check for updates at the top of the app. If a newer version is available,
choose Install. The installer downloads in the background, verifies its size and
SHA-256 checksum, saves your place, closes the player and restarts the new version.
If the download fails, the current app continues working. Update checks are manual;
ordinary listening does not contact the update server. No browser is opened.

YOUR DATA
Existing standalone recordings, collections, bookmarks and positions carry over.
Data remains in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ShiurPlayerStandalone\state.json.
Program files go in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\ShiurPlayer, separately from that data.
Recordings stay in the folder you selected. Keep their paths unchanged.
Setup and updates do not copy, edit or delete your audio recordings.
Earlier portable copies are left in place; use the installed shortcut afterwards.

LISTENING
Open audio folder once, then select a recording and click Play selected.
Every recording has its own position. Continue listening shows unfinished shiurim.
Positions save on pause, switching and normal close, plus periodic checkpoints.
Sudden power loss can lose the newest interval. Seek accuracy depends on the file.
Ctrl/Shift selects multiple recordings; Add to collection assigns them together.
Click a column heading to sort. The dividers cannot be dragged.

MINI PLAYER
Click Mini player, or minimize the main window. The smaller floating player has
Play/Pause, skips, speed and Open. Drag its edges to resize it; size and position
are remembered. Speed cycles 1x -> 1.5x -> 2x -> 0.5x -> 1x by default.
Settings offers the 0.5x alternative and optional shortcuts for use in other apps.
Closing the main window saves and stops playback. Minimizing keeps playing.

KEYBOARD
Space: play/pause. Left/Right: skip. Ctrl+F: search. Ctrl+O: audio folder.
Ctrl+B: bookmark. Ctrl+A selects the visible recordings when the list has focus.
Tab/Enter operate controls. Text fields keep their usual typing behavior.
Global shortcuts, when enabled: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P: play/pause; Left/Right: skip;
Up/Down: speed; PageUp/PageDown: recordings; 1/2/3: skip length; F: mini player.

PORTABLE OPTION
A separate portable ZIP is available for those who prefer it. Extract its entire
contents and run Shiur Player.exe. Its Check for updates can move you to the
installed version using the same saved data.

VALIDATION
Installer extraction, failed-update preservation and saved-data isolation were
tested using the shared Go installer code on Linux. Download integrity, metadata,
version checks and existing player controls were tested. Windows GUI executables
and embedded package contents were verified structurally. The actual Windows
setup UI, shortcuts, restart flow and speaker output remain untested here.
This is an unsigned per-user installer. Licenses are included with the app.

NEW IN 0.9.2
Unplayed and Finished views are in the sidebar. More and right-click
include Rename recording, Play next and Add to queue. Queue opens the editable play order.
Listening tools has A-B section repeat, jump to time, skip interval and sleep
timer presets, including pause at the end of the current recording.
Settings & backup exports/restores positions, collections and bookmarks to JSON.
Restore pauses audio and keeps a safety backup. Audio files are not included.
Ctrl+J jumps to time; Ctrl+M toggles mini player; in the list Ctrl+Q adds selected recordings to the queue. Mini progress is clickable.

NEW IN 0.9.2
Rounded violet-and-neutral interface throughout the app. Each recording remembers
its own speed. In the floating player, click minus to collapse to a tiny pill.
Hover over the pill to reveal skip buttons, or click the expand arrow to restore
its previous size. Drag empty space to move it. No extra software is required.

PLAYBACK PANEL
Click Collapse player on the main playback panel to free space for the library.
Play/pause and speed remain available. Expand player restores the full controls.
The choice is remembered. Tiny-player hover now has a short delay and stays
steady during clicks and dragging.
